# College essay and rewrite-origin coverage

Pre-ChatGPT US university coursework can supply hundreds of research controls for human false positives. The sources inspected did not supply a source-verified cohort of release-length US admissions essays with both human- and machine-origin rewrites.

## Existing evidence

Deep admits English documents of 300–2,500 words. Liang’s US college admissions essays are reserved for evaluation. ASAP and PERSUADE include secondary-school writing. The ordinary validation paraphrase stratum contains two machine-origin academic abstracts. The larger academic development check contains 66 machine rewrites, and three of four independently reserved native journal abstracts crossed the high cutoff under both v2 and the selected model. ## What the published pairings cover

- **Authentic admissions prompts.** A study with 49,804 archival 2018–2022 applicant essays generated approximately 650-word GPT-4 responses to the same prompt. The CC BY-NC 4.0 release includes the raw GPT-4 texts. The authors withhold the human texts. Fordham separately offers to discuss access to admissions records under a data-use agreement. This project has no such agreement. [Admissions study and data availability](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2025.100207) · [Authors’ release](https://osf.io/ywap5/) · [Fordham data-access statement](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-77847-z)
- **Published student rewrites.** OUTFOX contains 15,400 prompt/human/model triplets from the Kaggle Feedback Prize and releases model-text attacks. Its writers were US students in grades 6–12. Apache-2.0 is the repository license. Source-essay terms and overlap with the existing PERSUADE/ASAP family require their own check. [Pinned author release and source](https://github.com/ryuryukke/OUTFOX/tree/8dd6bfdec8e24ff6aeecae3015ab663f257e0350)
- **Both origin labels across domains.** LLM-DetectAIve includes machine-polished human and machine-humanized generated writing. Its student slice uses OUTFOX. The PAN task 2 archive aggregates 95,398 human-polished and 91,232 machine-humanized records across domains. The training schema gives text, language, class, source dataset, model and class description. Parent, assignment, paper and writer IDs are absent. Zenodo lists open access without a license field. The original DetectAIve repository supplies no corpus-wide data license. [Authors’ domain and transformation definitions](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-demo.35/) · [Pinned author repository](https://github.com/mbzuai-nlp/LLM-DetectAIve/tree/e92c588594c48c980d568244e67b99636f70bb58) · [PAN task 2 archive and class counts](https://zenodo.org/records/14966981)

A new coursework benchmark could reserve ETS writers before fitting, obtain the original prompts and permitted source materials, generate matched machine originals, then apply the same documented rewrite to both origins. Each original and every derivative would stay in one source family. A separate validation cohort of consented or appropriately licensed admissions writing would still be needed to measure that genre. No new benchmark, model fitting, or hosted compute forms part of this inventory.
